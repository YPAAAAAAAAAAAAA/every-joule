# Every Joule (phone reimplementation)

An original, from-scratch interactive physics toy about energy stores and transfers, inspired by **Every Joule - Lesson 1: Energy stores** by **Techartist** (Opus 5.5 + Three.js, 10 GCSE energy lessons): https://iamtechartist.com/every-joule/ and https://github.com/iamtechartist/every-joule-lesson1

The upstream lesson-1 repository has no open-source licence, so **no code, text, audio, fonts or assets were copied** - this is a clean-room reimplementation of the idea only. Written fresh and released under the MIT licence (see LICENSE). Three.js (MIT) is bundled under vendor/ with its own licence text.

Published as a GitBrew post. All credit for the original lesson series goes to Techartist.
